ABOMINATION
===========

Purpose
-------

Abominations use the Ritual Trophy system rather than conventional taming.

Unlike most creatures obtained through a ritual, however, an Abomination
does not immediately appear at its full size.

Instead, the ritual creates a small growing Abomination which develops
through several stages before reaching its adult form.


WAKING ROOTS
------------

The Abomination Ritual Trophy uses materials associated with the creature and
its Swamp environment:

    Abomination Trophy
    Root
    Guck
    Elder Bark

These materials are used as the basis for the ritual.

Vanilla does not explain exactly how Abominations are created, so this is not
intended as an explanation of their canonical origin.

The ritual simply provides a thematically appropriate way to reconstruct or
manifest one.


GROWING ABOMINATIONS
--------------------

An Abomination is exceptionally large compared with most creatures.

Because of this, having a fully grown Abomination appear immediately from the
ritual did not feel appropriate.

The ritual instead begins with a small Abomination at 25% of its adult size.

It develops through three growing stages:

    Stage 1: 25%
    Stage 2: 50%
    Stage 3: 75%
    Adult:   100%

Each growing stage uses a GrowTime of 1800 seconds.

Reaching that time does not guarantee progression to the next stage. The
current stage has a weight of 2 while the next stage has a weight of 1.

The Abomination may therefore remain at its current size for another growth
cycle before progressing.

This makes the total development time somewhat unpredictable rather than
giving every Abomination an identical growth duration.


BEHAVIOR WHILE GROWING
----------------------

Growing Abominations are already tamed and retain that state as they develop.

They cannot be fed or tamed again, and they cannot be commanded while they
are still growing.

Once the final stage develops into the normal adult Abomination, the creature
becomes commandable.


LORE AND INTERPRETATION
-----------------------

Vanilla presents the Abomination as a large root- and wood-like Undead
creature that emerges from the Swamp.

Vanilla does not establish a natural growth cycle or explain how an
Abomination comes into existence.

The growing stages are therefore an OTAB interpretation rather than
established Valheim lore.

They exist primarily because the exceptional size and physical nature of the
Abomination make gradual development more appropriate than immediately
manifesting the completed creature.


SUMMARY
-------

Abominations cannot be conventionally fed, tamed or bred.

They are obtained through a Ritual Trophy.

Unlike smaller ritual creatures, an Abomination begins at 25% size and grows
through several stages before reaching its adult form.

The growth stages are not intended to establish new Valheim lore. They are a
gameplay and presentation choice based primarily on the Abomination's
exceptional size.
