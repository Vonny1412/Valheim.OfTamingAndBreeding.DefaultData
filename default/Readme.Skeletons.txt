SKELETONS
=========

Purpose
-------

Skeletons are unusual because they appear throughout multiple stages of
Valheim's progression.

Vanilla represents them through several different prefabs that share the same
fundamental identity and appearance but are used at different stages of the
world.

OTAB preserves these Vanilla variants and connects them through one shared
Ritual Trophy.

This makes Skeletons both:

    one creature family

and:

    several progression-dependent Vanilla variants.


VANILLA SKELETON VARIANTS
-------------------------

The relevant Vanilla Skeleton progression consists of:

    Skeleton_Meadows
    Skeleton
    Skeleton_Swamps
    Skeleton_Mountains
    Skeleton_DeepNorth

These are existing Vanilla prefabs rather than OTAB-created replacements.

OTAB does not redefine their health, damage or other fundamental combat
statistics to create this progression. The existing Vanilla creatures are
used as they are.


SKELETON RITUAL TROPHIES
------------------------

Skeletons cannot be conventionally fed or tamed and cannot procreate.

Instead, they can be obtained through the Ritual Trophy system.

All of the relevant Skeleton variants share the same ordinary Vanilla
Skeleton Trophy.

The trophy therefore does not preserve which regional Skeleton originally
dropped it.

The ritual should not be understood as:

    "Restore exactly the Skeleton that dropped this trophy."

Instead, the shared Skeleton Trophy acts as the anchor for the Skeleton
family, and the exact Skeleton that manifests is selected separately.


A PROGRESSION-DEPENDENT RITUAL POOL
-----------------------------------

World progression determines which Skeleton variants can emerge from the
Ritual Trophy.

The available pool expands as additional Forsaken are defeated:

    Beginning:
        Skeleton_Meadows

    After Eikthyr:
        + Skeleton

    After The Elder:
        + Skeleton_Swamps

    After Bonemass:
        + Skeleton_Mountains

    After Fader:
        + Skeleton_DeepNorth

Earlier variants are never removed from the pool.

Each unlocked Skeleton variant has the same selection weight.

Progression therefore expands the possible results rather than guaranteeing
a stronger Skeleton.

Even after defeating Fader, a Meadows Skeleton may still emerge.


THE TROPHY DOES NOT REMEMBER THE BIOME
--------------------------------------

Because the Vanilla Skeleton Trophy is shared between the different variants,
the Skeleton used to create the Ritual Trophy does not determine which
variant may emerge from it.

For example, a trophy obtained from a Swamp Skeleton can eventually be used
in a ritual that produces a Deep North Skeleton if that variant has been
unlocked through world progression.

It may just as easily produce an earlier Skeleton variant.

The ritual operates on the Skeleton family rather than reconstructing the
exact prefab that originally dropped the trophy.


WHY BOSS KEYS ARE USED HERE
---------------------------

Boss keys are used to expand the Skeleton Ritual Trophy's result pool.

They do not determine whether the ritual itself can be performed.

A boss key therefore means:

    "This additional Skeleton variant can now appear."

It does NOT mean:

    "Skeleton taming is now unlocked."

This allows the possible ritual results to follow Valheim's world progression
without using boss progression as a hard requirement for obtaining Skeletons
through the ritual.


SUMMARY
-------

Skeletons form one creature family represented by several Vanilla progression
variants.

Because those variants share the same Vanilla Skeleton Trophy, the trophy
does not preserve the exact regional Skeleton from which it was obtained.

The Ritual Trophy therefore selects from a shared pool of Skeleton variants.

World progression gradually expands that pool, while earlier variants remain
possible.

Progression adds possibilities.

It does not guarantee a stronger Skeleton.
