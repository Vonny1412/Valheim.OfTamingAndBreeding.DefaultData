DVERGR
======

Purpose
-------

Dvergr are intelligent and initially neutral inhabitants of the Mistlands.

They are not treated as domesticated creatures. Feeding represents gradually
earning the trust of an individual Dvergr until it is willing to accompany
the player.

No specific Dvergr diet is established by Valheim. Their food preferences in
these data are interpretations based primarily on their combat roles and the
foods available around the Mistlands.


Dvergr Population
-----------------

Dvergr commonly inhabit towers, excavation sites and other structures in the
Mistlands.

Many of the Dvergr associated with these locations are not replaced after
they are killed. Some Dvergr Rogues can also occur through separate random
spawns, so Dvergr as a whole are not strictly limited to one-time spawns.

Allowing existing Dvergr to become companions provides another way to
preserve and make use of these inhabitants rather than simply fighting them.

Dvergr do not reproduce through these data.


Rogues
------

Dvergr Rogues are physical ranged fighters, normally using arbalests.

Their preferred foods therefore lean towards substantial Mistlands meals and
stamina-oriented mead.

Misthare Supreme is their most effective food, while Lingering Stamina Mead
also suits their combat role.

Tasty Mead is accepted by Rogues, but provides only a short feeding duration.

These preferences are not established Dvergr lore. They are interpretations
based on the Rogue's role and suitable foods available to the player.


Mages
-----

Dvergr Mages use the same general feeding concept but have different
preferences.

Their diet leans towards Eitr-related food and mead. Yggdrasil Porridge is
their most effective food, while Minor Eitr Mead also reflects their use of
magic.

Fire, Ice and Support Mages use the same diet. Their different spells are
treated as combat specializations rather than different biological or
dietary types.

As with the Rogue diet, these preferences are interpretations rather than
established Valheim lore.


Shared Foods
------------

Rogues and Mages also accept some of the same Mistlands foods.

This reflects them being members of the same culture despite their different
combat roles, rather than giving every Dvergr specialization an entirely
separate diet.

Tasty Mead is also accepted by both types, but is deliberately much less
effective than proper food and more specialized meads.


Relationship with Other Dvergr
-------------------------------

Becoming a companion does not make a Dvergr hostile towards other Dvergr.

A tamed Dvergr therefore retains its peaceful relationship with its own
group.

The player gains an ally without changing the Dvergr's identity or turning
its own people into enemies.


KVASTUR
=======

Kvastur is handled separately from ordinary Dvergr.

The Vanilla creature is a wooden companion of the Bog Witch. Its description
refers to a witch's companion being carved from sturdy wood, and its physical
properties and drops also identify it as a wooden construct rather than an
ordinary animal.


Ritual Trophy
-------------

Kvastur is obtained through a Ritual Trophy rather than conventional feeding.

The process is interpreted as reconstructing and reanimating or re-enchanting
a wooden construct.

Valheim does not explain the exact process by which Kvastur was originally
created or animated. The reconstruction represented by the Ritual Trophy is
therefore an interpretation.


Dvergr Faction
--------------

Despite belonging to the Bog Witch, Vanilla assigns Kvastur to the Dvergr
faction.

The reconstructed Kvastur retains this relationship and does not become
hostile towards Dvergr after joining the player.

The data do not assume that Dvergr created Kvastur. Vanilla does not establish
such a connection; only the faction relationship itself is retained.


Summary
-------

Dvergr are treated as potential companions whose trust can be earned with
food and mead rather than as domesticated creatures.

Rogues and Mages have different interpreted food preferences based on their
combat roles, while retaining several foods in common.

Kvastur uses a Ritual Trophy because it is a wooden construct rather than a
creature that would reasonably be won over through feeding. Its existing
Vanilla relationship with the Dvergr faction is preserved.
