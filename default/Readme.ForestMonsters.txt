FOREST MONSTERS
===============

Purpose
-------

This document records the design and lore reasoning behind OTAB's handling
of forest monsters. For now, it covers only Trolls.


TROLLS
------

Valheim does not clearly establish whether Trolls are capable of forming
a lasting bond with a player.

Given their enormous size, dangerous nature and place in the Black Forest,
OTAB does not interpret a tamed Troll as a fully loyal companion.

Instead, the Troll is seen as tolerating the player because it was fed
during taming and expects to continue receiving food afterward. Its
cooperation is therefore conditional rather than unconditional.

Trolls use TamedVersusPlayer: WhenHungry.

While fed, a tamed Troll tolerates the player. Once hungry, it becomes
hostile toward players again, despite remaining technically tamed.

This is an OTAB interpretation, not established Vanilla lore.
