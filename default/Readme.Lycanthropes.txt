FENRING, ULV AND CULTIST
========================

Purpose
-------

This document records the design and lore reasoning behind OTAB's handling
of Fenrings, Ulvs and Fenring Cultists.

Valheim does not clearly explain the exact relationship between these three
creatures. OTAB therefore expands upon the existing environmental lore
without attempting to establish a definitive canon explanation.


LORE INTERPRETATION
-------------------

Fenrings, Ulvs and Cultists appear to be closely connected to Fenrir and the
Fenrir cult found in the Frost Caves.

OTAB interprets them as different forms or ranks of the same curse rather
than three ordinary, unrelated animal species.

The approximate hierarchy used for this interpretation is:

    Ulv  ->  Fenring  ->  Cultist

This does not necessarily mean that one naturally grows into the next.
Instead, it describes their apparent degree of development.

Ulvs are the lowest and most animalistic form. They move on four legs, are
considerably weaker than Fenrings, and are kept as creatures or pets by the
Cultists in the Frost Caves.

Fenrings represent a higher form of the curse. They walk upright, are much
stronger than Ulvs, and roam the Mountains at night.

Cultists appear to represent an even more developed form. They retain an
upright and relatively humanoid form, use equipment and fire magic, and are
associated with the organized Fenrir worship found inside the Frost Caves.

The exact origin of any of these forms is deliberately left unexplained.
Valheim's environmental storytelling suggests transformation and Fenrir
worship may be involved, but does not provide enough information to define
a specific transformation process.


FENRING
-------

Fenrings can be tamed.

They are the only one of the three forms which naturally occurs in the open
world and can therefore be captured and brought home without changing
Valheim's dungeon mechanics.

Fenrings are allowed to procreate, but they cannot produce another Fenring.

Instead:

    Fenring + Fenring
            |
            v
         Ulv Cub
            |
            v
           Ulv

This is an intentional extension of the curse lore.

OTAB interprets the Fenring form as something the curse cannot reliably
reproduce through ordinary breeding. Two Fenrings are therefore unable to
produce offspring of their own rank. Their offspring falls back to the
lower, more animalistic Ulv form.

This also prevents the relatively powerful Fenring from becoming an easily
mass-produced creature while giving its unusual breeding behavior a lore
reason instead of applying an arbitrary gameplay restriction.


ULV
---

Ulvs can be tamed and are obtainable as the offspring of Fenrings.

Wild Ulvs normally exist inside Frost Caves. Creatures cannot leave these
dungeons because the cave entrances function as player-only transitions.

OTAB intentionally does not change this behavior. Adding special creature
transport through dungeon entrances would extend far beyond the scope of a
taming and breeding overhaul.

Fenring breeding therefore provides a lore-compatible way for the player to
eventually obtain the Cultists' Ulv creatures in the outside world.

The resulting Ulv Cub inherits the tame state of its parents. An earlier
design required newborn creatures to be tamed again before growing up, but
this was removed. The current interpretation is that although the curse
affects the offspring's physical form, it does not erase the trust inherited
from its tamed parents.

Adult Ulvs cannot procreate.

They are the lower result of Fenring reproduction and form a reproductive
dead end. This reinforces the idea that reproduction among these cursed
creatures is abnormal rather than ordinary animal breeding.


FOOD
----

Fenrings and Ulvs share the same diet:

    Lox Meat
    Bear Meat

This is intentional.

Large skeletal remains can be found throughout the Frost Caves, suggesting
that the creatures inhabiting them prey upon or consume large animals.

The exact species represented by these remains is not established, so OTAB
does not assume that the bones literally belong to Lox or bears. Lox Meat
and Bear Meat instead represent meat taken from suitably large prey.

Sharing the same food also reinforces the close relationship between Ulvs
and Fenrings.


FENRING CULTIST
---------------

Fenring Cultists are deliberately not tameable.

Unlike Ulvs, there is currently no satisfactory way to make Cultists
obtainable outside the Frost Caves without contradicting the hierarchy used
for the Fenrir curse.

Allowing:

    Fenring + Fenring -> Cultist

would mean that two Fenrings, which cannot even reproduce offspring of their
own rank, could somehow produce a considerably more developed form of the
curse.

This would contradict the reasoning behind Fenrings producing Ulvs.

Cultists also appear to be connected to Fenrir worship, ritual and possibly
transformation. If their form is achieved through such a process, biological
breeding would be an especially poor representation of their origin.

Valheim does not currently provide enough information about this process for
OTAB to implement a transformation or ritual without inventing substantial
new lore.

For that reason, Cultists remain outside the taming and breeding system.


SUMMARY
-------

    Creature         Tameable    Procreation    Offspring
    -------------------------------------------------------
    Fenring           Yes         Yes            Ulv Cub
    Ulv               Yes         No             -
    Fenring Cultist   No          No             -

The central rule is:

    The Fenrir curse can deteriorate through reproduction,
    but it cannot improve through reproduction.

A Fenring may therefore produce the lower Ulv form, but neither Fenrings nor
Ulvs can produce a higher form such as a Cultist.

This interpretation is not intended to define official Valheim canon.
It is an OTAB extension of the relationships and environmental storytelling
already present in the game, designed to preserve the identity of these
creatures while providing sensible taming and breeding mechanics.
