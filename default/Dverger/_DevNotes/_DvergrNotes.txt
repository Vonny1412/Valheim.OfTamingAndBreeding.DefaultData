DVERGR - DEVELOPMENT NOTES
==========================

These notes were used as reference when creating the Mistlands Dvergr data.


DVERGR TYPES
============

Dverger
  "Dvergr rogue"
  Biome: Mistlands
  Combat: Ranged (Arbalest)
  Max level: 3

DvergerMage
  "Dvergr mage"
  Biome: Mistlands
  More powerful due to magic
  Max level: 3

  Variants:
    DvergerMageFire
      Spells: Fireball, Clusterbomb

    DvergerMageIce
      Spells: Icebolt, Nova

    DvergerMageSupport
      Spells: Buff, Heal, Mistile

DvergerAshlands
  Stronger variant of the Dvergr rogue
  Max level: 3

INGREDIENT SOURCES
==================

Royal jelly
  Seeker brood (Mistlands)

Seeker meat
  Seeker and Seeker Soldier; strong enemies (Mistlands)

Jotun puffs
  Gathered and cultivated (Mistlands)

Magecap
  Gathered and cultivated (Mistlands)

Onion
  Cultivated (Mountains)

Carrot
  Cultivated (Black Forest)

Cloudberries
  Gathered (Plains)

Barley
  Cultivated (Plains)

Egg
  Chickens; purchasable after Yagluth is defeated

Chicken meat
  Chickens

Honey
  Meadows; very easy to obtain

Hare meat
  Hare; easy to obtain (Mistlands)

Blood clot
  Ticks; minions of Gjall (Mistlands)
  Gjall are strong enemies
