CALM BOSSES
===========

Purpose
-------

The actual bosses are deliberately left unchanged and are not made
tameable.

Calm Bosses exist as separate alternatives for players who would like to
keep a peaceful version of these imposing creatures without turning the
actual bosses into tameable creatures.

They are intended purely as prestige creatures.


Separate Creatures
------------------

A Calm Boss is not the original boss after being defeated, converted or
tamed.

It is a separate creature based on that boss.

No additional lore is assumed to explain their existence. Their purpose is
primarily mechanical and cosmetic: they provide a peaceful boss-like
creature while leaving the actual boss encounters and their identity
untouched.


Prestige
--------

Calm Bosses provide no special progression role.

They are collectible prestige creatures for players who want a peaceful
counterpart to a boss.

Their value lies in obtaining and keeping such an unusual creature, not in
turning Valheim's bosses into ordinary tames.
