FULINGS
=======

Purpose
-------

Fulings are not treated like ordinary tameable animals.

They are intelligent, social beings with their own settlements, hierarchy
and culture. OTAB therefore interprets their taming less as domestication
and more as cooperation bought through valuables.


Valuables Instead of Food
-------------------------

Fulings can carry Coins as loot in Vanilla.

OTAB takes this as inspiration for interpreting them as creatures with an
interest in valuables. Instead of ordinary food, Fulings are therefore fed
with valuable items such as Amber, Amber Pearls, Rubies and Silver
Necklaces.

More valuable offerings keep them fed for longer.

This is an OTAB interpretation. Vanilla does not establish that Fulings can
actually be bribed or tamed with valuables.


Being Fed
---------

The normal fed state has a special meaning for Fulings.

Being fed represents that the Fuling has been sufficiently bribed and is
currently willing to cooperate with the player.

While fed, a tamed Fuling is willing to fight wild members of its own group
on the player's behalf.

Once it is no longer fed, this temporary cooperation against its own kind
ends. The Fuling remains tamed, but will no longer turn against wild Fulings
for the player.


Different Fulings
-----------------

Regular Fulings and Fuling Archers are comparatively easy to win over and
remain fed for longer.

Fuling Berserkers and Fuling Shamans require considerably more time to tame
and become hungry more quickly, making their cooperation more expensive to
maintain.

All of them use the same basic bribery concept.


No Breeding
-----------

Fulings do not use OTAB's procreation system.

They are a people rather than livestock, and their reproduction is
deliberately left outside the taming and breeding mechanics.


Summary
-------

Fulings are not won over through food. They are bribed with valuables.

Their fed state represents an active arrangement with the player, and while
that arrangement lasts, they are even willing to fight wild Fulings on the
player's behalf.
