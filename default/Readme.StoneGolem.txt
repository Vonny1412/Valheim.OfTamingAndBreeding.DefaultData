STONE GOLEM
===========

Purpose
-------

Stone Golems use the Ritual Trophy system rather than conventional taming.

Because of their size and physical nature, a Stone Golem does not immediately
appear in its completed form.

Instead, the ritual begins a gradual reconstruction process in which the
Golem is assembled through several stages.


GROWING STONE GOLEMS
--------------------

The Growup system is used mechanically to represent the gradual
reconstruction of a Stone Golem.

The reconstruction passes through three incomplete stages:

    Stage 1: 25%
    Stage 2: 50%
    Stage 3: 75%
    Adult:   100%

Each stage lasts 300 seconds before the next result is determined.

Unlike the Abomination, however, progressing through these stages is not
guaranteed.


RECONSTRUCTION CAN FAIL
-----------------------

At each stage, the Stone Golem has two possible outcomes:

    Continue reconstruction:
        Weight 1

    Crumble back into a Stone Golem Trophy:
        Weight 1

The reconstruction therefore has a 50% chance to continue at each stage and
a 50% chance to fail.

All three stages must succeed for a complete Stone Golem to emerge.

This gives the entire reconstruction process a 12.5% chance of succeeding
from beginning to end.

If reconstruction fails, the incomplete Golem crumbles and leaves behind a
normal Stone Golem Trophy.

The trophy can therefore be understood as the remaining anchor of the failed
reconstruction rather than the entire ritual simply disappearing.


GROWTH VERSUS RECONSTRUCTION
----------------------------

Although the Growup component is used mechanically, the Stone Golem is not
intended to be biologically growing.

The increasing size represents the Golem being progressively reconstructed.

This also distinguishes it from the Abomination.

The Abomination develops slowly but does not normally lose its progress.

The Stone Golem reconstructs much more quickly, but every stage carries a
significant risk of collapse.


SUMMARY
-------

Stone Golems cannot be conventionally fed, tamed or bred.

They are obtained through a Ritual Trophy and reconstructed through three
incomplete stages before reaching their full size.

The reconstruction is considerably faster than the Abomination's growth, but
it can fail at every stage.

A failed reconstruction crumbles back into a normal Stone Golem Trophy,
allowing the remains of the attempt to be recovered.
