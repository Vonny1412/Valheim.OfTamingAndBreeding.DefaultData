MISTLANDS CREATURES
===================

Purpose
-------

The Mistlands contain several creatures whose biology and relationships are
only partially explained by Valheim.

Where possible, these data use details found in the creatures themselves,
their environment and their behavior as the basis for taming, feeding and
reproduction.

Some of the resulting lifecycles are interpretations rather than established
Valheim lore. These interpretations are documented below.


Hares
-----

Hares use ordinary breeding and produce Leverets, which eventually grow into
adult Hares.

Their diet consists entirely of plant-based foods.

Carrots are included partly because of the familiar association between
rabbits and carrots. Jotun Puffs provide a more direct connection to the
Mistlands, while the remaining foods provide additional suitable plant-based
options.

Valheim does not establish the natural diet or lifecycle of Hares in detail.
The Leveret and its growth into an adult Hare are therefore straightforward
extensions of ordinary animal biology rather than established Valheim lore.


TICKS AND GJALL
===============

Ticks and Gjalls have one of the clearest visible biological relationships
among the creatures of the Mistlands.

Gjalls carry Tick Eggs beneath their bodies and can release them during
combat. These eggs produce independent Ticks.

Valheim establishes this direction of the relationship:

    Gjall
      |
      v
    Tick Eggs
      |
      v
    Ticks

The wider lifecycle and the exact nature of the relationship between the two
creatures are not explained.


Ticks and Soft Tissue
---------------------

Ticks are fed with Soft Tissue.

Ticks can be encountered around the enormous petrified remains found
throughout the Mistlands, where Soft Tissue can still be extracted from the
remains.

This is interpreted as the Ticks being attracted to and feeding upon the
remaining Soft Tissue.

Valheim does not explicitly state that this is why Ticks gather around these
remains.


Tick Exudate
------------

Fed Ticks can produce Tick Exudate.

Tick Exudate is an invented substance and has no established equivalent in
Valheim lore.

It represents some undefined secretion or other substance produced by a
well-fed Tick. Its exact biological nature is deliberately left unexplained.


Gjalls and Tick Exudate
-----------------------

Gjalls are attracted to Tick Exudate and consume it.

Why they desire this substance is deliberately left undefined.

It could represent some form of nutrient, secretion, scent, reproductive
signal or something stranger. No particular explanation is assumed.

This creates an interpreted cycle around the relationship already visible in
Vanilla:

    Gjall
      |
      | produces
      v
    Tick Eggs
      |
      | hatch
      v
    Ticks
      |
      | when fed
      v
    Tick Exudate
      |
      | attracts / feeds
      v
    Gjall

Only the relationship from Gjalls to Tick Eggs and Ticks is directly
established by Valheim.

The return from Ticks to Gjalls through Tick Exudate is an interpretation
created for these data.


Independent Ticks
-----------------

Ticks produced by a Gjall are independent creatures.

A tamed Gjall therefore does not automatically produce tamed Ticks.

Although the Tick Eggs are visibly carried beneath the Gjall, the resulting
Ticks are separate creatures and are not treated as part of the Gjall itself.


Summary
-------

The Mistlands creatures use several biological relationships suggested by
their environment and Vanilla behavior.

Hares use a simple animal lifecycle.

Ticks are interpreted as being attracted to the Soft Tissue found within the
petrified remains of the Mistlands.

Gjalls already produce Ticks in Vanilla. Tick Exudate extends this established
relationship in the opposite direction, creating a speculative lifecycle
between the two creatures without defining exactly how their alien biology
works.
